## 💡 How to Run in Google Colab

1. **Select a Unit:** Navigate to the table above and click the **Open in Colab** badge corresponding to the notebook you want to explore.
2. **Environment Setup:** Once Colab launches, sign in to your Google Account.
3. **GPU Activation (For Units 18–20):**
   * Go to `Runtime` ➔ `Change runtime type`
   * Under **Hardware Accelerator**, select **T4 GPU** or **GPU**
   * Click **Save**
4. **Execute Cells:** Press `Shift + Enter` or click the **Play** icon to run cells sequentially.
